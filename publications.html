Science Communication

Publications & Digital Media

VC

Vidyarthi Chekumuki

JVV's children's science magazine, described in the project brief as having launched in 1990, aimed at sparking scientific curiosity among students.

JV

Janavignanam

A digital platform for science explainers and scientific biographies, extending JVV's science communication online.

NN

Nammakam–Nijam

Literature intended to examine common beliefs through evidence and scientific reasoning — the name translates roughly to "Belief & Truth".

AV

Chemistry in Daily Life & Audio-Visual Learning

Educational audio content and classroom presentation resources, including the "Chemistry in Daily Life" audio learning series.
