Chekumukhi Examination Instructions

50Questions
4Options Each
EN / TELanguages
60 minDefault Duration
The 60-minute duration and the scoring scheme below are prototype defaults, configurable in js/exam.js, pending official confirmation by JVV.

Scoring

Correct answer: +1 mark. Incorrect answer: 0 marks. Unanswered question: 0 marks. No negative marking. Maximum possible score: 50 marks.

How to take the test

  • 1Each question offers four answer options, shown in English and Telugu together by default. You can switch to English-only or Telugu-only using the language selector at any time without losing your answers.
  • 2Select an option by clicking anywhere on its row. You can change your selection as many times as you like before submitting.
  • 3Use the Previous and Next buttons, or click any number in the question palette, to move freely between all 50 questions.
  • 4Use Mark for Review on any question you want to revisit later. Marked questions are highlighted in the palette.
  • 5A countdown timer is visible at all times. When it reaches zero, your answers are submitted automatically.
  • 6Click Submit Test when you are ready. You will see how many questions are answered and unanswered, with a chance to confirm or go back.
  • 7After submission, your answers are locked. You can view your score and a full answer review immediately afterwards.

Demonstration limitations

This is a frontend-only prototype. The timer runs in your browser and is not tamper-proof, there is no server-side verification of your identity or score, and refreshing the page will attempt to resume your current attempt rather than silently starting a new one. None of this should be treated as a secure, official examination delivery system.
