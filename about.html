About Us

Jana Vignana Vedika

What is Jana Vignana Vedika?

Jana Vignana Vedika (JVV) is a people's science-awareness organisation. Its work centres on promoting scientific temper, rational thinking, and evidence-based reasoning among the general public, and on supporting science education among school students through initiatives such as the Chekumukhi Science Talent Test.

JVV encourages people to examine claims — including superstitions and unverified beliefs — through observation, evidence, and testable reasoning, rather than accepting them on faith or tradition alone.

  • Promotion of scientific temper and rational thinking
  • Public science education for communities and schools
  • Evidence-based examination of superstitions and unsupported claims
  • Community participation in science awareness activities
  • Student-centred science learning, including Chekumukhi
Our Philosophy

Why scientific temper matters

Question, Don't Assume

JVV encourages people to ask for evidence behind claims, rather than accepting them simply because they are widely believed or traditional.

Learn By Doing

From Chekumukhi questions to Bala Melas and mobile science labs, JVV favours hands-on, practical science learning over rote memorisation.

Community Participation

Science awareness works best as a shared public effort — involving students, teachers, volunteers and the wider community together.
