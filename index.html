What is Chekumukhi?

A science talent test built to spark curiosity

Chekumukhi means "inquisitive" — and that is exactly the spirit of this talent test. It invites school students to apply scientific concepts to everyday situations rather than simply recall facts, while celebrating scientific temper and rational thinking at every stage.

Applied scientific thinking

Questions connect classroom science to real-world situations, encouraging logical reasoning over rote memorisation.

Bilingual by design

Every question and option appears in both English and Telugu, so no student is held back by language.

Open and immediate

The demonstration examination is available right now — no waiting period, no access restriction in this prototype.

About JVV

A people's movement for scientific temper

Jana Vignana Vedika (JVV) is a science-awareness organisation that promotes rational thinking, public science education, and the evidence-based examination of superstitions. Through the Chekumukhi Science Talent Test and many other initiatives, JVV works to bring the spirit of scientific inquiry into schools and communities.

  • Promotion of scientific temper and rational thinking
  • Public science education for all age groups
  • Evidence-based examination of superstitions and unverified claims
  • Student-centred science learning and community engagement
